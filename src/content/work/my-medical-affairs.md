summary: Figma to WordPress build with a fast custom search and social sign-ups sent to HubSpot.
role: Lead developer, from Figma to launch and ongoing improvements
stack: [WordPress, Hello Elementor, PHP, JavaScript, HubSpot]
problem: My Medical Affairs needed its Figma design turned into a site where medical-affairs professionals find events quickly and register with Google or LinkedIn, and the team needed every registration in HubSpot.
approach: I built the site from scratch on Hello Elementor with reusable templates, tuned for desktop, tablet and mobile. I wrote a custom search in PHP and JavaScript that returns results as you type, and a script that picks up each Google or LinkedIn registration and sends it to HubSpot as a contact.
result: Visitors find events as they type, and every social sign-up appears in HubSpot as a lead with no manual data entry.
