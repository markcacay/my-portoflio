---
title: Delivery slots that know every postcode
client: Getfish, seafood from the Sydney Fish Market (AU)
summary: A new custom theme from Figma, and a checkout that only offers delivery times that reach the customer.
role: Lead developer, theme rebuild and checkout logic
stack: [WordPress, WooCommerce, PHP, JavaScript, Iconic Delivery Slots API]
problem: Every postcode Getfish delivers to has its own delivery times, but their delivery-slot plugin could only attach one postcode to each slot, so customers at checkout saw times that didn't apply to them.
approach: I rebuilt the theme from the agency's Figma design. Then I added custom fields so each slot can hold many postcodes, and wrote a checkout script that reads the customer's postcode and asks the plugin's API for the right slots.
result: Customers only saw delivery times that actually reached them, and the team managed postcodes from the WordPress dashboard. Getfish has since moved to Shopify, so the screenshots are from the archived 2023 site.
