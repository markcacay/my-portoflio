title: A pageant voting platform I built and run
client: Pageant Poll (my own product)
summary: My own SaaS product. Online pageant voting where fans sign in with Facebook and buy extra votes.
role: Founder and sole developer
stack: [Laravel 10, Vue 3, Inertia.js, PrimeVue, PayPal, Xendit, PayMongo, Docker]
problem: Pageant organisers in the Philippines needed an easy way to run online fan voting, and fans needed to vote and support their favourite candidate from their phones.
approach: I built a Laravel and Vue app where organisers create a pageant and its candidates, and fans sign in with Facebook to cast free daily votes. Fans who want to do more buy supporter packs through PayPal or local gateways like Xendit, PayMongo and Maya. An admin panel tracks sales, expenses and affiliates, and the app runs in Docker.
result: Organisers set up and run their own polls, and one app handles sign-in, vote limits, payments and sales reporting.
