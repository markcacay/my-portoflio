title: An online pageant voting platform, built end to end
client: Pageant Poll (PH)
summary: A Laravel and Vue SaaS for online pageant voting, where fans sign in with Facebook and buy extra votes.
role: Full-stack developer, from database to deployment
stack: [Laravel 10, Vue 3, Inertia.js, PrimeVue, PHP 8.2, MySQL, PayPal, Xendit, PayMongo, Docker]
problem: The client wanted a platform where pageant organisers in the Philippines could run online fan voting on their own, and fans could vote and support their favourite candidate from their phones.
approach: I built a Laravel and Vue app where organisers create a pageant and its candidates, and fans sign in with Facebook to cast free daily votes. Fans who want to do more buy supporter packs through PayPal or local gateways like Xendit, PayMongo and Maya. An admin panel tracks sales, expenses and affiliates, and the app runs in Docker.
result: Organisers set up and run their own polls, and one app handles sign-in, vote limits, payments and sales reporting.
