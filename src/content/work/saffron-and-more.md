stack: [WordPress, WooCommerce, PHP, Klaviyo, Facebook Pixel]

approach: I built the site from a blank theme using the agency's Figma design, set up WooCommerce with custom wholesale and retailer accounts, added the Facebook Pixel, and connected Klaviyo with an abandoned-cart flow.
result: Wholesale and retail customers shop the same store at their own prices, and abandoned carts get an automatic Klaviyo follow-up.
